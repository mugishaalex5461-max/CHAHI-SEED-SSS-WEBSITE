Academics

Our Academic Program

CHAHI SEED follows the Ugandan National Curriculum with rigorous academic standards. Our comprehensive program is designed to develop critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving skills alongside strong subject knowledge. We maintain small class sizes to ensure personalized attention and support for each student.

Curriculum Streams

Science Stream

Comprehensive science education emphasizing practical skills and theoretical knowledge.

Subjects Include:

  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Biology
  • Mathematics
  • Computer Science
  • English

Arts Stream

Broad humanities education developing critical analysis and communication skills.

Subjects Include:

  • Literature
  • History
  • Geography
  • Economics
  • Social Studies
  • English

Commercial Stream

Business-focused education preparing students for commerce and entrepreneurship.

Subjects Include:

  • Accounting
  • Business Studies
  • Economics
  • Mathematics
  • Computer Science
  • English

Core Subjects

These subjects are mandatory for all students across all streams:

English

Development of communication, analytical, and creative writing skills through literature and language study.

Luganda

Study of local language, culture, and national identity through Luganda language and literature.

Mathematics

Strong foundation in mathematical concepts essential for academic and professional success.

ICT

Digital literacy and computer skills for the 21st century workforce.

Physical Education

Sports and fitness development promoting health, teamwork, and leadership.

Religious Studies

Moral and spiritual development nurturing values and ethical living.

Teaching & Learning Methods

Student-Centered Learning

Active participation and engagement in learning process with teachers as facilitators.

Practical Laboratory Work

Hands-on experiments and practical activities in well-equipped science laboratories.

Digital Learning

Integration of technology and online resources to enhance learning and research.

Group Work & Projects

Collaborative learning through group projects developing teamwork and communication skills.

Research & Innovation

Encouragement of independent research and innovative thinking beyond textbooks.

Personalized Support

One-on-one tutoring and mentoring for students needing additional support.

Assessment & Evaluation

Continuous Assessment

Regular tests, assignments, and classwork throughout the term to monitor progress.

Mid-Term Exams

Comprehensive examinations in the middle of each term to assess understanding.

Final Exams

End-of-term examinations and national exams (PLE, UCE, UACE) evaluating overall performance.

Feedback & Reporting

Regular feedback to students and parents through report cards and parent-teacher conferences.
