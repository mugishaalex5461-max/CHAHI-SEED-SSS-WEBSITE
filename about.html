Our School

CHAHI SEED SECONDARY SCHOOL is a premier educational institution located in the scenic Kisoro District on the slopes of Mount Muhabura in Uganda. Since our establishment, we have been committed to providing quality, holistic education that develops students academically, spiritually, and morally.

Our History

2010

School Founded

CHAHI SEED SECONDARY SCHOOL was established with a vision to provide quality education in the Kisoro District.

2012

First Graduation

The first cohort of students graduated successfully, marking a significant milestone in our journey.

2015

Campus Expansion

Major infrastructure development including new science laboratories and sports facilities.

2018

Digital Transformation

Introduction of ICT facilities and digital learning platforms to enhance education quality.

2020

Excellence Recognition

Recognized for academic excellence and community service by regional and national education bodies.

2024

New Era

Launch of advanced programs and enhanced student support services for better learning outcomes.
