Admissions

Join Our School Community

Welcome to CHAHI SEED

We welcome applications from qualified students for admission to all levels. Our admission process is transparent, fair, and based on merit and demonstrated ability. We seek students who are academically capable, disciplined, and committed to contributing positively to our school community.

Senior One Admission

Timeline

  • Application Deadline: Rolling admissions
  • Entrance Exam: As per school schedule
  • Results Release: Within 2 weeks of exam
  • School Opening: January/July

Requirements

  • Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) results or equivalent
  • Birth certificate or national ID
  • Medical/health report
  • Recommendation letter from previous school
  • Entrance examination results

Senior Five Admission

Timeline

  • Application Deadline: As announced
  • Entrance Interview: Scheduled basis
  • Document Verification: 1 week after interview
  • School Opening: January/July

Requirements

  • Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) results
  • Birth certificate or national ID
  • Medical/health report
  • Recommendation letter from previous school
  • Subject selection form

Scholarships & Financial Aid

We believe education should be accessible to deserving students regardless of financial constraints. CHAHI SEED offers various scholarship and financial aid programs:

Merit Scholarships

Full or partial scholarships for academically excellent students based on entrance examination performance.

Need-Based Aid

Financial assistance for qualified students from low-income families.

Sports Scholarships

Opportunities for talented athletes to study while representing the school in sports.

Arts Scholarships

Support for students with exceptional talents in music, drama, and other arts.

To Apply: Contact the school office for detailed scholarship application procedures and requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

When are admissions open?

We accept applications throughout the year on a rolling basis. Major intake periods are January and July.

What are the admission requirements?

Requirements vary by level. Generally, you need previous school records, entrance exam results, and medical report.

Do you offer boarding facilities?

Yes, we offer safe and comfortable boarding facilities with 24-hour supervision and care.

What subjects are offered?

We offer a comprehensive curriculum including Sciences, Arts, and Commercial studies. See Academics page for details.

Are there scholarships available?

Yes, we offer merit-based, need-based, sports, and arts scholarships for qualified students.

How can I apply?

Download the application form from our website, complete it with supporting documents, and submit to the school office.
