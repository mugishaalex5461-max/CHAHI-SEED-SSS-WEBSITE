Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time to visit the school?

You can visit during office hours (Mon-Fri, 8 AM - 4:30 PM). For scheduled tours, please contact the admissions office in advance.

How long does it take to receive a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to inquiries within 24 hours during business days. During holidays, responses may take longer.

Can I schedule a campus tour?

Yes! Contact our admissions office to schedule a guided campus tour at a time convenient for you.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, mobile money (MTN, Airtel), cash, and cheques. Contact the finance office for details.
